This logo is perfect for the "Iron Man / Stark Industries" vibe you mentioned earlier. It screams high-tech, energy, and precision. The color scheme is a classic "Teal & Orange" cinematic look, often used in sci-fi to represent advanced technology (Teal) and raw power (Orange).

Here is a breakdown of the specific palette and how to use it in your Astro/Tailwind project to keep that "dashing" look.

The "Fusion Energy" Palette
The trick with this logo is that it uses gradients, not flat colors. To match your website to the logo, you shouldn't just pick one blue and one orange; you need to map them to specific UI roles.

1. The Backgrounds (The Void)
Don't use pitch black (#000000). It’s too harsh. Use a "rich black" or deep charcoal that matches the dark parts of the logo's structure.

Deep Void: #09090b (This should be your bg-background)

Armour Grey: #18181b (For cards, sidebars, and panels)

2. The Primary Brand Color (Intelligence)
Use the Cyan/Teal from the left leg of the "A". This represents the "AI" and "Tech" side of your startup.

Electric Cyan: #06b6d4 (Tailwind cyan-500)

Glow Highlight: #22d3ee (Tailwind cyan-400 - use this for hover states)

3. The Accent Color (Power)
Use the Orange/Gold from the right side. This is your "Action" color. Use it sparingly for "Sign Up" buttons or critical alerts.

Fusion Orange: #f97316 (Tailwind orange-500)

Molten Gold: #fb923c (Tailwind orange-400)
